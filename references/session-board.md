# Sessions & Boards

Methodology reference for the high-level meetings where management surfaces from operations to plan the future — **tactical sessions** and **quarterly board preparation**, adapted for a **PM of a direction**. Consumed by `quarterly-planning`. Framework terms (SMARTCBP, ROAIP, LAC, AIDA, GTD, acquisition-channel hypotheses) are the plugin's working vocabulary.

> A PM of a direction runs the same rhythm at their scale: a **tactical session** with the team before each quarter, then a **quarterly readout** to stakeholders/leadership. The numbers below are guidance, not hard rules.

---

## The session & board rhythm

| Type | Cadence | Horizon / purpose |
|------|---------|-------------------|
| Operational session | yearly, 3–5 days, intense | Concrete plan for the year: goals, plans, top-priority tasks → a clear annual plan. |
| Tactical session | quarterly (×3/yr), ~1 day | Correct the tactical plan (3–6 month horizon); review tasks & statuses. Held **before** each quarterly board. |
| Quarterly board / readout | quarterly, after the tactical session | Compact readout of what the session produced, to stakeholders/leadership. |

The tactical/operational sessions **prepare** the readout: work with the team first, then report.

---

## Tactical session — structure & roles

**Ground rules:** everyone fully focused (no parallel phone/laptop); an "information vacuum" — clean minds, no rushing off. Productivity depends on team sync, preparation (pre-work the heavy questions), agenda quality, the moderator, and the secretary.

**Roles:**
- **Moderator** — organizes and steers; holds focus on the agenda; draws everyone in; manages timing; ensures results are captured (who/what/when).
- **Secretary** (second-most-important role) — keeps an **ARCV** actionable follow-up (`references/communication-frameworks.md`); hard-insists on clearly fixing each decision and reads it back so nothing is lost; every idea consciously goes into work or is dropped. For online — record and transcribe.

**Preparation:**
1. **Agenda** (template): list of questions + schedule; per topic — duration, who prepares, who is needed which day; include lunch breaks.
2. **Pre-session survey**: participants log strategic, "karmic", and painful questions ahead of time. Mix closed and open questions ("What could the direction start/stop doing so you get maximum satisfaction / maximum results? What would make the product grow faster?"). If it's the first session or long overdue — survey everyone, not only leads.

**During the session:**
1. Start each day by reviewing yesterday's notes.
2. Day one: assign the moderator and secretary.
3. Keep a **task sheet** from the follow-up: (1) task titles by the rules (perfective verbs, essence in the title — `references/communication-frameworks.md`); (2) checklist; (3) Definition of Done; (4) responsible.

**After the session:** finalize the task sheet (all tasks, owners, links to the tracker, status — tasks added only after landing on the idea sheet); collect session feedback (value to me / value to the org / organization quality); write tasks out to the standard (title → why/what/how → checklist → owner → priority/deadline).

## Quarterly board / readout — preparation checklist (PM of a direction)

Bring **worked-through** questions — each with the owner's proposal and argument, ideally the answer already; the responsible manager picks and recommends the option.

1. **Follow-up statuses from the previous board** — all prior decisions should be done; track the **GTD coefficient** (target ≥ 80%).
2. **Planned vs actual, previous period** — budget/plan vs fact for the period, full-year forecast at current trend, deviations + causes. If the revenue/goal plan is missing — show the brainstorm results on how to catch up immediately.
3. **Strategic & operational goals** — status, year-end forecast; if deviating — why and how it's being solved; if a goal changed — the detailed reason; new goals + the resources for them. Headline goals as **SMARTCBP**; report progress as **3T5F** (`references/reporting-3t5f.md`).
4. **AI / innovation for cost reduction** — concretely in money, in a table (hours, money value, what it enabled: didn't hire / freed / same team does 2× more). Idea profitability via **ROAIP** (`references/roi-frameworks.md`).
5. **Product/health metrics** — for B2B, the **LAC** table (CSAT or analogue; Adoption Rate; LTV; ideally EDC — Expansion/Downgrade/Churn — and cross-sell/recommendation signals). For a product direction, substitute the direction's funnel and NPS/love-hate themes.
6. **Acquisition-channel hypotheses** (scalable channels) — a channel is "found" when you know: (1) the ICP; (2) how you reach prospects; (3) how you interest them (**AIDA**); (4) how you get them to pay. Present with statistics, required investment, and a forecast. **Don't** call un-costed organic a "channel"; when a channel is found, scale it immediately (escalate for budget if needed).
7. **Questions for approval** — worked through, with proposal and argument.

> A CEO/PM may debate a task *during* the board, but **not after** it lands in the board follow-up.

## Typical errors
- Never surfacing from operations (no sessions at all).
- Parallel phone/laptop work; no information vacuum.
- Sessions longer than ~5 days (people burn out) — move the rest to tactical sessions.
- A weak secretary (next-day notes nobody can parse; lost ideas).
- Bringing un-worked questions to the board without a proposal/argument.
- Debating tasks after they enter the board follow-up.
- Arriving at the board without a prior tactical session with the team.
- Calling un-costed organic a "channel"; not scaling a found channel immediately.
