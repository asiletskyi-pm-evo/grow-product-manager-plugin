# Epic — PROJ-77

## Goal

Seller working hours in the cart, on the web and in the mobile app, so that a shopper knows when the seller will answer.

## Scope

- the hours of each seller under its block in the cart;
- a notice when the seller is closed now;
- the hours in the order confirmation e-mail.

## Out of scope

- delivery slots (a separate epic).
